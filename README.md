## What Does AB-210 Cover?

AB-210 focuses on understanding how AI can be used to support sales activities in Microsoft Dynamics 365. Candidates should become familiar with concepts related to sales productivity, customer engagement, opportunity management, and AI-assisted decision-making.

A strong understanding of Dynamics 365 Sales is helpful when preparing for this exam because many topics are connected to practical sales scenarios.

## Why Is AI Important for Sales Teams?

Sales professionals often work with large amounts of customer information, leads, opportunities, and communication data. AI can help organize this information and provide useful insights.

AI-powered capabilities can help sales teams:

- Identify important sales opportunities
- Prioritize activities and prospects
- Improve customer interactions
- Save time on routine tasks
- Make better data-driven decisions
- Understand sales pipeline trends

Learning how these capabilities work can help candidates develop practical knowledge rather than simply memorizing exam information.
